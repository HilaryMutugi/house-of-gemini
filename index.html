Common questions

Ordering made simple.

Ask a question
How do I place an order?

Message us on WhatsApp or use the order form. Tell us what you need, how many pieces and when you need them, and we will guide the next step.

What should I send for a uniform quote?

Your quantity, the sizes or age groups, your logo and colours, and your deadline. Photos of what you wear now help too.

Can you make custom designs?

Yes. Share your idea, a sketch or a reference photo and we will work out the right fabric, fit and finish with you.

Do you work on the coast?

Yes. We are based in Nairobi and serve the coast from Mariakani in Kilifi County.

Can you put our logo on the uniforms?

Yes. We offer embroidery and heat press branding, so your logo is part of the design from the start.

Can we reorder the same uniform later?

Yes. Repeat orders are part of how we work, so your next batch can match the first.
